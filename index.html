Reminders

  • Automation is required! You must build an automated loop to have any hope of success. Luckily it's easy: select the Train, click Add Schedule, and add Stops in a loop near Mines, Turrets, and the Base.
  • The initial Train is sitting on Tracks. Extend new Track off of that Track to make your loops.
  • To make a Train Stop loop, repeat the first Stop as the last Stop to finish the Loop (e.g. A1 to A2 to A3 to A1).
  • Build new Trains by first selecting the (B)ase building and click the button for the Train type that you want.
  • Mines must be adjacent (one tile away) from Train Stops to be Mined successfully. Likewise, Turrets must be adjacent to a Stop to get their Energy refilled by a Train.
  • You can build a Combat Train that shoots automatically as it moves; it must be refueled with Energy only at the Base, so be sure to have a Stop at the Base for it.
  • You can Pause the game to catch your breath.
  • Turrets and Combat Trains will attempt to neutralize Hives in addition to Creeps.
  • Be sure to build at least one Energy Mine and one Construction Material Mine so that you don't soft lock yourself.
  • Hive activity will slowly grow stronger over time.
  • Trains loaded with Construction Material will pause for one second to auto Repair damaged buildings and Track within one tile as they travel.
  • You can Salvage depleted Mines to clear space.
