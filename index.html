Energy warning

Turret / artillery out of energy

A turret or artillery building ran out of energy. Both turrets and artillery must be supplied with energy by a build/mine train loaded with energy and stopped at an adjacent non-destroyed train stop.

Track warning

Train track destroyed

Some of your train track has been destroyed. Trains carrying construction material will automatically rebuild it if they can, but note that you can also manually rebuild track with the 'Build track' button, laying it from healthy track to where the destroyed track is.

Neutralizer note

Use gates with walls

If you build walls surrounding your base, note that gates can be used to let neutralizers (blue ally units) pass through so that you can keep a solid defense of walls while allowing neutralizer units to leave. Creeps cannot pass through gates.
